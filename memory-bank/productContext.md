# Product Context

## Problem Statement
While LLMs are powerful, organizations lack a simple, secure way to connect them with existing business tools. Current solutions are either:
- Locked into specific ecosystems (OpenAI, Claude)
- Too technical (LangChain, AutoGPT)
- Not seamlessly integrated with LLMs (Zapier, Workato)
- Still in experimental phase (MCP-based tools)

## Solution
WayStation provides:
- No-code integration hub for connecting LLMs to business tools
- Enterprise-grade security and compliance
- Vendor-agnostic approach
- Simple configuration without engineering resources
- Seamless workflow integration

## Target Users
Primary:
- Information workers using AI assistants
- Enterprise teams seeking AI automation
- Business users without technical expertise

Secondary:
- IT administrators managing tool integrations
- Compliance officers overseeing AI usage
- Development teams building on the platform

Comparable to Identity and Access Management (IAM) market:
- Okta's projected FY2025 revenue: $2.6 billion
- Growing demand for AI integration solutions
- Enterprise shift toward AI automation
